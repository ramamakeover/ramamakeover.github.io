A few facts about head lice

  • Head lice are most common in young children and their families.
  • They spread mainly through head-to-head contact. They can't jump or fly.
  • Having lice is not a sign of being unclean.
  • Don't share combs, brushes or towels.
  • If one person at home has lice, check everyone you live with.
  • Scratching can cause sores. If you're not sure it's lice, or treatment hasn't worked, ask a pharmacist or doctor.

Sources: NHS – Head lice and nits · CDC – Head lice
