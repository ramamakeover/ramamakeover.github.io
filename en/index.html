Head lice care

Lice care, without the embarrassment.

Head lice are a common problem and can happen to anyone. There is nothing to be embarrassed about. Rama Makeover offers a head lice removal service for children and adults. Call or WhatsApp us to ask how the service works and what it costs.

Ask about lice care9372448675

A few facts about head lice

  • Head lice are most common in children, especially school-age children.
  • They spread mainly through direct head-to-head contact.
  • Having lice is not a sign of being unclean.
  • Avoid sharing combs, brushes, towels and pillows.
  • If one person at home has lice, check everyone else in the family too.
  • If the scalp has sores, swelling or severe itching, see a doctor.
